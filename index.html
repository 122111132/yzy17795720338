Story

From a lens, to small actions, to medicine.

I picked up a camera before I knew where the path would lead. Noticing the stray cats in my neighborhood, I started paying attention to small lives that most people walk past — and I kept coming back. That habit of noticing became everything.

01 · Volunteer

Animal Care Station

At a local animal care station, I volunteered in daily care routines and general support. There I learned that a small life's wellbeing depends on consistent, everyday attention.

02 · Internship

Hospital Internship

A hospital internship gave me firsthand exposure to a clinical environment, showing me how structured medical care works in practice.

03 · Advocacy

Social Media for Strays

I share posts on social media advocating for stray animal protection, hoping to turn individual care into public awareness.

04 · Feeding

Neighborhood Stray Cats

Feeding stray cats in my neighborhood became a steady routine — small visits, simple food, and the quiet work of helping animals at their most basic welfare.

Why Medicine

These experiences taught me that compassion needs a method. I am drawn to veterinary and biomedical science because it can turn my care for small lives into the knowledge and skill to protect them properly.
